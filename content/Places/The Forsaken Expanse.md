A large expanse of desert located in the northeastern [[Tanglewood Peninsula]].

The histories say that the great desert was once called the Verdant Dragonfield. It was a great expanse of lush green meadowlands, beloved by the elves. The legends say that the Verdant Dragonfield was poisoned by the dying breath of the great black dragon Timphiliax, as he fought [[Barbanus]].
