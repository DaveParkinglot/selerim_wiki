# Overview
The most flammable town in the northeast has seen it's share of troubles, but it's still going strong! The town developed from a couple of small farmsteads into regional agricultural center and minor trade hub servicing nearby [[Wyrmrest Keep]], surrounding villages, and the occasional adventurer heading into the wilds.

# Attributes
- **Appearance**: This town is almost completely made of wood. The outskirts consist of small farmers huts, with larger buildings appearing as you head further into town. At the center is an old stone arena, which is used nowadays as the town square and market.
- **Economy/Resources**: The dragon at [[Wyrmrest Keep]] is an ever present threat, but the village pays regular tribute to be allowed to remain unburnt. Besides that there are only a few small neighboring settlements but the town and the entire region really is on the edge of unexplored wilds.
- **Dangers/Secrets**: The dragon at Wyrmrest Keep is an ever present threat, but the village pays regular tribute to be allowed to remain unburnt. Besides that there are only a few small neighboring settlements but the town and the entire region really is on the edge of unexplored wilds.
- **Points of Interest**: The most interesting thing about this place is it's proximity to Wyrmrest Keep. Shops are slightly better supplied here compared to nearby settlements due to local trade, but it's still quite rustic.

# Hooks
- The villagers pay tribute to the dragon at Wyrmrest Keep. It's a humiliating arrangement but what can the townsfolk of the most flammable village in the world do?
