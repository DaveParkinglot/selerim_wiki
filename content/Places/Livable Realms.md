- The select few pockets of Selerim that can support sapient life, they are akin to bubbles of safety within a raging storm. 
- Most of these realms are free from the deadly arcane fallout of the [[Worldbuilding/Events/Nameless War]].
- The unlivable regions of the world have never been mapped because they are both uninhabitable and deadly to those who tread there.
- The scope and number of the Livable Realms is not yet known.
- The largest and most notable of these Livable Realms is thought to be the [[Places/Inhabitable Lands]].
