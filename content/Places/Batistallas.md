# Overview

Batistallas is the largest city under primary control of the [[Batista Family]] within the [[Tiialia|Kingdom of Tiialia]]. Founded by veterans from the Band of Batista, a mysterious company of soldiers who arrived toward the end of the siege of the [[Corrodreth|Salted City]]. They were gifted this land for their support in the final days of the siege, and the city has since grown rapidly. However, this land also rests at the base of the [[Bleeding Peaks]], and is at times visited by haboobs composed of the dark arcane fallout sweeping down from the mountains.

# Attributes

- **Appearance**: Hazy, cluttered, industrial, with a large manor for the Batista family on the eastern half and strewn with statues to “The Eternal Batista”
- **Economy/Resources**:
  - [[Haunted Oil]] (a flammable black viscous material drawn from the foothills of the Bleeding Peaks with medicinal and arcane properties)
  - Soldiers for the Tiialian army
- **Dangers/Secrets**:
  - Zalvidar Batista himself
  - The Bleeding Peaks
- **Points of Interest**:
  - Abandoned yuan-ti structures litter the outskirts of the city.
  - BT\&T Arena is where large gladiatorial fights and Batistaball tournaments are held.
  - Zalvidar Batista Lordship Library is an official library dedicated to the writings and rare artifacts of Lord Batista.
  - Batista Royal Manor is the primary seat of the Batista family, although it seems they often prefer to escape to their country estates in the foothills of the Bleeding Peaks.
  - The Carousel Club, owned by Dean Jacksons, is the grooviest, hippest spot in town and also probably run by the mob.

# Hooks

- Rumors abound that Zalvidar Batista, the lord of the city and head of the Batista family, is an undead or immortal being.
- The city is home to Reunion Tower, an ancient, 100 story monstrosity filled with nightmares on every level plus a gift shop, observatory, and rotating restaurant on floor 89.
- The occasional haboobs of dark arcane energy that sweep down from the Bleeding Peaks offer unpredictable effects on the citizens and surrounding area of the city.
