- Former ruling family of the Kingdom of [[Places/Tiialia]], they ruled from the capital city of [[Places/Nova Jersaeria]].
- The king of [[Places/Tiialia]], Antonio Adonzio, was the patriarch of the Adonzio family before his death, which probably ended their line.
- The Adonzios allied with [[Factions/The Kingdom of Tiialia/Landucci Family]] after the Midsummer Massacre, in what became known as the Adonzio Accord.
- The elderly, infirm King Antonio Adonzio was killed by [[NPCs/Villains/Lord Draco Landucci]] in the Nova Jersaeria throne room, just before Lord Landucci himself was slain by [[Player Characters/The High Rollers/The High Rollers]].
