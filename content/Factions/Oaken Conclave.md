The governing council of [[Mitlin]] consists of the Wise Wizards and local elected citizens.

[[Melvin Oaksleeves]] is the head wizard and represents Mitlin's progressive faction. [[Sheldyn Elaris]], chief representative of the [[Dragon Faithful]], is the leader of the conservative faction.
