An infamous noble family of [[Factions/Kingdom of Tiialia/index|Tiialia]] led by [[Faustia Fanto-Domingo]] and Nico Fanto-Domingo. Faustia, a cunning enchantress, was part of [[Lord Draco Landucci]]'s plot to rule through the puppet king Adonzio.

Ultimately, [[Faustia Fanto-Domingo]] was killed for her treachery, and Nico Fanto-Domingo was imprisoned by [[Antonino Esposito|King Antonino Esposito I]] after his coronation.
