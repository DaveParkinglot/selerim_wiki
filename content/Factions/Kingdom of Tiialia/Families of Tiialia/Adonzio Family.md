Former ruling family of the [[Factions/Kingdom of Tiialia/index|Kingdom of Tiialia]].

During the [[War of the Four Families]], the Adonzios allied themselves with the [[Landucci Family]] after the Midsummer Massacre, the event that wiped out the [[Esposito Family]]. Their alliance became known as the Adonzio Accord.

As the broker of the peace, the patriarch of the Adonzios, Antonio Adonzio, became king of [[Factions/Kingdom of Tiialia/index|Tiialia]]. He ruled for 25 years until his death at the hands of the traitorous tyrant [[Lord Draco Landucci]] in 264.
