Before his death, the patriarch was [[Lord Draco Landucci]], High Commander of the Legions of [[Factions/Kingdom of Tiialia/index|Tiialia]]. He was supposedly King Antonio [[Adonzio Family|Adonzio's]] closest ally, though Draco was secretly using him as a puppet with the help of [[Faustia Fanto-Domingo]].

Draco Landucci had 2 twin sons named Lorus and Korus, who were renowned warriors. He also had a failson named Montius "Monty" Landucci.

[[Lord Draco Landucci]] and his twin sons Lorus and Korus were killed by [[Player Characters/The High Rollers/index|The High Rollers]] and [[Antonino Esposito]] after their plot was discovered.
