- [[Morganth]] himself once tried to summon demons in the heartland of Tiialia ([[Adventures/01 Dunland's Raiders\|Dunland's Raiders]]), though it is unclear if he is a warlock, a sorcerer, a wizard, or some other form of spellcaster.
