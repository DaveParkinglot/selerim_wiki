- A _comically_ evil group who serve the mysterious arcanist [[Morganth]].
- [[Morganth]] himself once tried to summon demons in the heartland of Tiialia ([[Dunland's Raiders]]), though it is unclear if he is a warlock, a sorcerer, a wizard, or some other form of spellcaster.
- During the adventure [[Morganth's LEGION OF DOOM]], a group of Morganth devotees infiltrated the Tomb of Zalaash, beneath the ruins of [[Corrodreth]]. There, they located and retrieved a [[Blackfyre Orb]] for Morganth.
