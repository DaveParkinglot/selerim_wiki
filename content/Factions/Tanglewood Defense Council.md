A joint defense council led by the three most established settlements on the [[Tanglewood Peninsula]]: [[Crabbley]], [[Crillville]], and [[Mitlin]]. Crabbley and Crillville first joined forces in the late summer of 264 after unrest caused by [[Tanglers]]. Mitlin joined soon after, because the sanctity of their town was threatened by undead after the Aldwood Lighthouse catastrophe.
