The Tanglers were an organization of druids led by the [[Malmstein the Tangler]]. They opposed the expansion of settlements onto the [[Tanglewood Peninsula]], and resorted to extremism.

Most were originally members of the [[Cult of the Crab]], but they split over ideological differences. They were labelled as eco-terrorists after their attack on [[Crabbley]]'s annual [[Crab Fest]] in 263.
