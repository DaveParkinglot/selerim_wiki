After [Selvetarm](https://5e.tools/deities.html#selvetarm_drow_scag) eternal banishment to the Infinite Abyss, his surviving followers fractured and retreated beneath the surface into the Underdark. Only in recent times have these drow remnants on Selerim united under a single banner, calling themselves the Vlorn Dynasty.
