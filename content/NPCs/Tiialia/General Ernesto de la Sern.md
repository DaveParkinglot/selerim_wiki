# Overview

General Ernesto de la Sern is [[Alejandro Ruz]]'s top general and confidant. Although born to a wealthy, noble family, General de la Sern has become a strong believer in Ruzian thought, and has helped build the Ruzian Revolutionary Guard into a formidable force.

[[Player Characters/The Stacked Deck/index|The Stacked Deck]] found out that he had secretly been coordinating with forces from [[Vlorngroth]] to counterbalance the [[Batista Family]]'s potential alliance with the [[Legion of Doom]].
