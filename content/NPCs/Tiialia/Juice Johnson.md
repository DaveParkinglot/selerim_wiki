# Overview

Juice Johnson has the juice. If you live in [[Tiialia]] and need the juice, he has it. He is often hired to cook for royal and high prestige events, and has a chain of hot sauce focused restaurants across Tiialia.

## [[The Great Tiialan Heist]]

[[Player Characters/The Stacked Deck/index|The Stacked Deck]] first encountered Juice while he was serving as a hired chef for Roberto Batista's birthday party. He ignore the adventurers as they snuck through the kitchen on their way to save [[Alejandro Ruz]]

## [[Voyage a la Isla Ruz]]

The Stacked Deck next encountered Juice at his establishment in [[Nova Jersaeria]], Juice Johnson's Juice and Jambalaya. They took some of his juice for the road.

## [[The Assassination of King Antonino Esposito by the Coward 'Playing Card' Murphy]]

The [[Knight of Confidence]] later encountered Juice Johnson in [[Batistallas]], where he warned him about [[Batista Family|Batista]] plans to have a patsy take the fall for a fake assassination of [[Antonino Esposito|King Antonino Esposito]].
