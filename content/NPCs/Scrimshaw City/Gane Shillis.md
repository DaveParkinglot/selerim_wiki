# Overview
- Gane is [[Bobby Dismorphia]]'s older cousin.
- Like Bobby, he grew up in [[Borfield]], but unlike Bobby he hit the big time through his stand-up comedy.
- He now does comedy shows in big cities like [[Nova Jersaeria]] and [[Scrimshaw City]]. The latter is where he spends most of his time.

## [[Bobby's Bachelor Birthday Bender]]
- Gane was invited to Bobby's Bachelor Birthday Bender by [[Hoary Boulder]].
- During the party, his soul was taken by the demon lord [[Pazuzu]] to join "Pazuzu's Pals" and do stand-up for them. 
- The boys made a deal to get Gane's soul back on weekends, but they have to complete Pazuzu's quest.
