# Overview
- Private security sellsword who was hired to guard [[NPCs/Scrimshaw City/Arno Rickshir]]'s empty Boldriver Pawn Shop while he ran his traveling magic shop in the [[Places/Tanglewood Peninsula\|Tanglewood]].
