# Overview

Krampus is an archfey who resides mainly in the [[Yuleshade]], within the Feywild. As an archfey, Krampus' primary domain is the enforcement of laws and moral contracts. He ensures that pacts are honored, and punishes those who break their pacts. Though he punishes pact breakers severely, he does believe in redemption.

Traditionally, on [[Giftmas]] Eve, Krampus travels the planes to gather pact breakers. He offers redemption to some, but the worst of the bunch are thrown in his black sack of punishment to be tormented until the next [[Giftmas]], when they will have an opportunity to redeem themselves.

## [[Kindlethicket - The Winter Hunt]]

Krampus, as always, took pact breakers from the material plane into his black sack of punishment. This year, however, he relented, allowing [[Pudding]] to go free after being petitioned by brave adventurers.

## [[Selerim 5K]]

Far into the future, Krampus seemingly has gained dominion over an entire astral system. His system may have affiliation with [[Deep Rock Corporation]], as the [[Back to the Ship|gingerbread-infested Deep Rock Mining Outpost #7781]] was located within the Krampus System.
