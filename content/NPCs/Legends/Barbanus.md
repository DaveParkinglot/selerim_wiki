# Overview

Before the onset of the [[Generations' War]], Barbanus was called Barbanus the Bodacious. He was lord of Bastion, a town on the edge of the Verdant Dragonfields. His keep, then called Barbanus' Bastion, was built of shimmering white cloudstone gifted to him by the cloud giants of [[Bonogon Peaks]]. Presumably he had a good relationship with the giants of the peaks.

In these times, the histories speak only of Barbanus' killing of the great black dragon Timphiliax. This event began the [[Generations' War]], which claimed countless lives and tore the civilizations of the [[Summer Lands]] down to their roots.
