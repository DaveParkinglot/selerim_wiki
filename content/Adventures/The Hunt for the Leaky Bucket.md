# Adventure Premise

- The old, grizzled ship captain [[Captain Winchester Bone|Winchester Bone]] has heard rumors that his long-lost ship—the Leaky Bucket—has been spotted hanging in the overgrowth of the [[Tanglewood Peninsula|Tanglewood Wilds]].
- He is seeking a group of adventurers to navigate the Tanglewood and locate the ship for him, and your adventuring party has contacted him about taking the job.

# Outcomes

- [[Crabbley]]'s newest adventuring party met with Winchester Bone for an impromptu interview at the Crab Claw Inn.
- Captain Bone was impressed with the group's abilities, and gave them the job. He especially liked that Crodd Farmington was familiar with the story of the Leaky Bucket and its disappearance.
- The party trekked through the Tanglewood using their excellent survival skills, though Shrimba Scrampi had a tough time surviving on the surface and was bitten by a sneaky snake.
- During their travels, Alfee Treespring befriended a fey frog named Sir Toadington. He assisted with navigation, atop his trusty steed, a 2 1/2 foot tall Tanglewoodcock.
- After successfully locating the Leaky Bucket, which was hanging nose down in the jungle canopy, the group was surprised by a swarm of giant wasps that were living in the ship.
- Alfee Treespring inadvertently knocked the Leaky Bucket loose from the vines holding it in place after using a Thunderwave spell to finish off the giant wasp infestation on the ship.
- In the captain's quarters, the group located Winchester Bone's prized pantaloons, and Ernester used the Mending spell to fix the many holes in them. This is likely to get them a bigger reward from Captain Bone.
