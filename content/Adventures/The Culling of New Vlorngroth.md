# Adventure Premise

Rebellion stirs within the conquered city formerly known as Fordsfaire. These lowly dissenters continue to be a stake in the heart of newly appointed lord mayor, [[Count Calloway Duskveil]]. If the city is to live up to its updated name— [[New Vlorngroth]], then these rebels must be purged from the city… permanently.

As the Count's most faithful familiars, you have been tasked with infiltrating a secret rebel meeting on the auspicious eve of the [[Revenancer's Revel]], a drow celebration of necromantic magic.

For this mission, you will be working under the thumb of your manager [[Morbus]], who will be available to chat on sending stone.

# Outcomes

"Morbus' Minions" burst into a Church of Lathander, expecting light resistance per the comments of their handler, [[Morbus]]. They stumbled into a trap and were attacked by two knights and a priest. The priest, Hamson, grew grotesque leathery black wings and flew out of the church after his two knights were killed. He left behind an array of strange artifacts and offerings to the "Imprisoned One".

After some back and forth on their sending stones with Morbus and Jonathan in research, they investigated the church and realized that Hamson had been seeking to make a deal with an evil god cleanse the city of the [[Vlorn Dynasty|Vlorn]] invaders. They sought out a way to locate Hamson and found a profane ritual in the basement, and a vrock. After Klaus put the vrock to sleep with a spell, they looted the ritual's spell components and discovered that the deal Hamson had made was with in fact with the demon lord [[Pazuzu]], aka the Imprisoned One. When the group contacted Morbus to tell of their success clearing out the church and the basement, he was very surprised. When they pressed for more information from Jonathan in research, he was fired from his job by Morbus. The group realized that Morbus, that devious cur, had betrayed them, and had clearly set them up to die in the church raid.

At this point, the group debated the merits of siding with the priest Hamson and turning the tables on Morbus. There was much dissent, but Bombus' argument won the day, which amounted to "if we all stick it out, we'll get to be management some day and we'll get to betray underlings to their death!". With their decision to back up their manager made, the group used the ritual spell components they found to track down Hamson through the dark, rainswept streets of New Vlorngroth.

They tracked Hamson to the Foundry District, where he was gathering the iron needed to summon more minions of Pazuzu. They fought off a swarm of iron shrikes and brought the fight to the priest Hamson himself, defeating him in the nick of time. Before they killed him, Morbus confirmed over sending stone that Hamson had made a deal with the Pazuzu to cleanse the city of the Vlorn. Hamson tried to convince the group to side with him against their manager, claiming that "Pazuzu is always looking for more pals", but he was unsuccessful in this. Ultimately, Hamson was slain on the street. The group of dutiful familiars then retired for the night after another successful mission cleansing rebels. They may have received holiday pay on account of it being [[Revenancer's Revel]] Eve, but Morbus certainly disputed their hazard pay.
