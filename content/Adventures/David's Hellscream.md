# Adventure Premise

Much has happened. Much is happening. It's a big world, so why not make some ice cream?

# Outcomes

Several days after returning from the feywild, [[Bobby Dismorphia|Bobby]], [[Squeembo Montgomery|Squeembo]], and [[David Hellscream]] all met up with [[The Earl]] on the quad at [[White Grove]]. [[Quarf Lustigoff|Quarf]] was debating students at the time, but became violently ill after a student protestor threw a dung pie into his open mouth. Unfortunately this caused him to miss the grand adventure. David wanted to make "David's Hellscream", a spicy ice cream. He decided he wanted to make it using manatee milk and dragon peppers.

Luckily, The Earl disclosed that his father [[The Duke]] kept an aquatic pocket plane in his study which would contain manatees. The boys traveled his [[The Duke]]'s house, but found him mysteriously missing. They activated some traps while snooping around, but eventually they located the pocket plane in the study.

In the aquatic pocket plane, the boys set about hunting down manatees to milk. They fought off sharks and multiple traps, eventually stumbling into the ancient prison of an aboleth. The aboleth nearly killed Squeembo by stealing his memories, but the boys managed to seal him back into his prison. Ultimately, they recovered 9 gallons of manatee milk and an ancestral dwarven greatsword named "Dusk".

At the end of the day, the boys returned to White Grove where David made his first batch of "David's Hellscream". The consensus was that it wasn't very good ice cream. Maybe he just needs to find a different ingredient...
