# Adventure Premise

Although many disparate reaches of space remain untamed, shackled by criminal syndicates or stunted by remnants of Selerim’s capitalist past, the United Coalition of Planets has brought peace and order within the territory of its large and growing collective of member systems. However, the specter of conflict still looms large, whether from the warrior-centered and honor-bound J’talith Hegemony on the eastern rim of the galaxy or the conniving and isolationist Pomerian Empire circling the galactic core. Can the Coalition truly hope to tame the final frontier?

Near the Demarcated Systems, bordering J’talith and Pomerian space, the UCP starship Chuhm (pronounce Choom) has detected a distress call from within an asteroid field just outside of Coalition space while on a routine survey mission. Strangely, the universal translators seem to be having trouble deciphering specifics of the call, indicating an ancient or extragalactic origin. Although travel into the Demarcated Systems is typically prohibited as it risks sparking conflict with the Coalition’s neighbors, the UCP admiralty has ordered the Chuhm to investigate. What begins as a nonroutine response to a mysterious distress call will eventually become known as The Kalimar Incident.

You are a junior officer on the UCP Chuhm. In addition to creating a level 3 character, you should designate a division for your character (preferably most or all PCs will have a different division):

- Command (specializing in leadership and tactical training)
- Security (specializing in maintaining order and responding to potential threats)
- Engineering (specializing in mechanical systems and maintaining ship functionality)
- Sciences (specializing in sensors, research, and theoretical and physical laboratory work)
- Medical (specializing in biological studies and medicine)

# Outcomes

**Part I: The Distress Call**
Captain Archibald Halcard: "Stardate 26092.4. While on a routine survey mission near the Demarcated System, we have picked up a mysterious distress call from just outside of Coalition space. Although we have struggled to translate the call, the admiralty has ordered us across the Demarcation Line to investigate the source. In addition to my senior bridge crew, I am joined by the following junior officers (the players each shared their story).

Crew, I want us to approach this call with caution—we don’t want to draw the attention of the J’talith or Pomerians, but we _also_ need to avoid the many asteroids in this field. However, it wouldn’t hurt either to begin deciphering the language of the distress call—any efforts on this front could help us in breaking down specifics of the call itself. I defer to our bridge crew as we make our way across the Demarcation Line into this asteroid field."

Crew at each of their stations did a skill challenge to determine progress as the ship traverses the asteroid field. They chose to focus on helping to navigate, trying to avoid detection, or assisting in translating the message.

1. Chag attempted to translate the message and failed, their position is now probably revealed to any other ships in the sector.
2. Franky translated the message by transferring power (it is a distress call).
3. BatGPT navigated by interlinking with the computer.
4. Ernard helped navigate by going to the steering wheel (that was not a steering wheel, but the computer followed his commands).
5. Chag, knowing their position is detected, shouted out a message to try and deceive any hostile ships that they are actually a Fontanos party barge (critical miss).
6. Franky did a history check to see about the asteroids' movement and successfully got them to navigate around the asteroids.
7. BatGPT decrypted the title of the distress call (it came from the ancient Yetari civilization).
8. Ernard attempted a nature check on this ship and fails.
9. Chag gave Number One a drink while doing a medical check to put her at ease.
10. Franky finished the description of the message.
11. BatGPT tried to steer and fails.
12. Earnard used history to try and decrypt further.

The UCP Chuhm steered around a large asteroid, and was briefly caught up in the flare from a neighboring red dwarf star. As the flare subsides and the crew focuses their eyes, the sheen of ancient metal across the surface of a mysterious vessel slowly came into view. The design was unlike anything that any of them had seen before, and clearly was ancient in origin.

![[z_assets/ancient_yetari_vessel.png|200]]
_The ancient Yetari vessel in the asteroid field_

The vessel appeared to align with that of the Yetari civilization, an ancient, warlike empire that stretched across far reaches of the Primary Quadrant tens of thousands of years ago. Although its hull appeared battered, the computer indicated a muted power source still emanated from deep within the ship. If that source could be amplified, the distress call could be more comprehensively analyzed. The captain ordered an away team composed of the four ensigns. However, they could not beam onboard because the environmental readings cannot discern the inside of the ship, so they entered through a breach in the hull via a shuttle craft.

On board, the crew sought to find the source of the distress call, and to restore power to the derelict ship. They encountered a J'talith war party, who charged at them with the battle cry of “T’waaaagh!” (which translated to “Glory to the God of Battle may his spirit shine upon me as I achieve glorious honor today in battle”). The Commodore was a buffoon and had charged ahead, separating him from the main party, leading to a difficult fight. They battled the J'talith warriors and their P'tark, defeating them all except the J'talith Strategor who escaped out of a hatch. They restarted the life support system. They also found an ancient Yetari key artifact on the ship.

With life support restored, they found the source of the distress beacon, an ancient Yetari computer console. They downloaded the location of Kalimar, the long sought after homeworld of the Yetari, onto a data chip. However, the ship then entered a self-destruct mode!

The crew ran to escape as debris crashed down around them, and Yetari Detention Sentinels reawakened to stop them. Even a J'talith unproven warrior that had escaped the previous battle stood in their way. By working together and freeing each other from the trappings of the Yetari Detention Sentinels, the crew managed to escape the ship right before it exploded.

Back aboard the UCP Chuhm, the crew recounted their story and the captain uploaded the coordinates of Kalimar into the computer. With approval from the admiralty, the UCP Chuhm descended further into the Demarcated Systems to discover the ancient Yetari Homeworld.

**Part II: Heir of the Yetari Empire**
The ensigns, reveling in their success on their first away mission, were told by the captain to deliver the Yetari device to Chief Science Officer Valt. They stopped by the Lounge and Cultural Center to celebrate along the way. Chag realized that he had signed up for the open mic night to talk about Fantanos, but he failed to win any new adherents. Franky and Ernard were accosted by other ensigns interested in learning more about their exploits. Franky then went to deliver the Yetari device to Chief Science Officer Valt, only to realize that one of the ensigns had snatched it from him! She turned out to be a Pomerian spy. The ensigns intercepted her and recover the device, but she then said that the Pomerians had already hacked the UCP Chuhm's systems and knew exactly where Kalimar was located. The spy was confident that the Pomerians would beat the UCP to the secrets of Kalimar, Yetari device or no!

![[z_assets/open_mic_night.png|300]]
_Chag fails at open mic night at the Lounge and Cultural Center_

The UCP Chuhm arrived at Kalimar, and the ensigns were teleported to where sensors indicated the device to connect with the Yetari artifact would be located. However, the ensigns were instead transported to a locked room, where a group of Pomerians had also been trapped. After a standoff situation, the two parties decided to work together to escape the room. They decided to press a button in the middle of the room, which initiated a countdown timer. Eventually, the decided to let the time count down to zero, at which point the lights came and the doors opened. The Pomerian crew decide to go the way that that UCP crew were not going, saying that they would beat them to the Yetari secrets.

![[z_assets/countdown_challenge.png|300]]
_The Pomerians and the UCP crew part ways after succeeding the countdown challenge_

The crew then encountered a room with a mysterious series of markings, similar to the countdown markers from the previous room. Bat GPT realized that some tiles in white are prime numbers, and then began jumping on only prime numbers. The crew found out the the prime numbered tiles were the tiles that they could land on without triggering a trap. All crew members found the math aspect of this room to be exhilarating and perhaps the most rewarding of all challenges that they had encountered and overcome.

![[z_assets/yetari_tile_puzzle.png|300]]
_The incredible prime numbers trap room, a favorite for all involved_

The party moved into the core platform of the Yetari temple, and found a computer in a central platform with a place to fit the Yetari device. As the Pomerian crew approached from the other direction, they inserted the Yetari device. By inserting the device, they awakened a Yetari Visage, who bade them to take up their place as the heirs of the Yetari Empire: "Brave children of the stars. Welcome. Hear my voice! One voice, many faces. Does this better suit your expectations? (the Visage took various forms of gods in which the crew members believed) It is I. ...The journey you took to reach me could not have been an easy one. It seems you are the first to find me, although it appears you have company. I am the voice of the great and mighty Yetari Empire. Your awakening of my visage can only mean that the cycle of rebirth is upon us, and the Yetari must again claim our domain over the stars!"

The Visage gave them a button, which they indicated that, when pressed, would unleash a virus on the Pomerian ship in orbit. At the same time, they gave the Pomerians the same button. The Yetari Visage indicated that they wanted a willing accomplice in their rebirth. Chag threw the button over the ledge of the temple immediately before any decisions could be made, and the Pomerians locked eyes with the UCP crew, nodded, and threw their button device over the ledge as well. Battle commenced!

![[z_assets/yetari_vestige.png|300]]
_The Yetari Visage_

Together, the crew and the Pomerians held off the Yetari Detention Sentinels and pummeled the Yetari Visage. They realized that they could destroy the computer that birthed the visage to destroy the visage itself, and proceeded to do so. This caused the visage to explode into nothingness, cursing the failings of these supposed heirs of the empire.

The ensigns returned back to the UCP Chuhm. Captain Halcard and the Pomerian captain exchange words about how their might be an opportunity for peace between their two peoples. The ensigns celebrate back at the Lounge and Cultural Center, having survived a fantastical first two away missions. And yet, the admiralty requires all evidence of the Kalimar Incident (as it was come to be known) to be redacted, so as to deter future scavengers to Kalimar and cover up the Pomerian entente.
