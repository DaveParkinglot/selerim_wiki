## Adventure Premise

Rumor is spreading across the [[Tiialia|Kingdom of Tiialia]] that [[Antonino Esposito|King Antonino Esposito I]] has been murdered in [[Batistallas]] by "Yi-Ti" belonging to some reborn [[Corrodreth]]. Others say that the king WAS a snake guy! A few even claim that it wasn't the king at all that was murdered, but an impersonator... Few are sure of where the truth lies. However, you know the truth (or some shred of it)—shared to you by a dying Ambassador “Tiny” Timmy Trebles--that King Antonino Esposito is being held captive by [[Lord Zalvidar Batista]] in his manor at the footsteps of the Bleeding Peaks for some sinister, impending purpose.

It looks like it is all up to you, and perhaps some familiar friends, to save the king and maybe even save (or destroy) Tiialia itself. Will you take up the call to save the king?

## Outcomes

In his dying, gabagoul-scented breath, Ambassador "Tiny" Timmy Treble informed the Stacked Deck that Zalvidar Batista is part of a dastardly plot in cahoots with the [[Legion of DOOM]] and aligned Neo-Corrodreth fanatics. [[Player Characters/The Stacked Deck/index|The Stacked Deck]]'s actions in killing the fake yuan-ti King Antonino Esposito and his cohort has perhaps upended a key element of their plans, whatever they may be. But it has also created a scene of chaos. Word is spreading across Tiallia that the king has been killed, or perhaps the king was a yuan-ti? Or maybe a yuan-ti was impersonating the king? However, the Stacked Deck know at least the partial truth, communicated to you by Ambassador Treble—the real King Antonino Esposito is being held captive by Zalvidar Batista at his estate in the foothills of the **Bleeding Peaks**. Using directions given to them by the Grassy Knoll, The Stacked Deck make haste toward the estate atop the royal steeds you requisitioned from the fake king’s carriage. As they approach the estate on the outskirts of Batistallas, they see the violet bleed becoming ever thicker about them, and they feel a sense of doom. What’s more, over the roar of thunder coming from the distant peaks they also hear quite the commotion.

As they enter through the open front gate to the estate, they pass by multiple slain yuan-ti, drow, and Batista guards. As they approach the estate, they see an enormous swirling, violet portal that seems to be consuming the very estate itself. And engaged in in a flurry of activity in front of the portal are none other than a contingent of Ruzian freedom fighters, led by [[Alejandro Ruz]] and [[General Ernesto de la Sern]].

The players discover Alejandro Ruz and company trying to close and explode a portal to the Legion of Doom

“My friends, what a relief to have your assistance! It seems that our Batistan friends have initiated a portal to the Legion of DOOM through which they can begin their invasion of Tiallia. Unfortunately for them, we were ready! We have been shipping vast quantities of infernal iron to Isla Ruz over the past year—enough to build an Infernal Bomb. We believe Zalvidar Batista himself is within the portal, seeking to bridge Batistallas with an invading Neo-Corrodreth army via a conduit island in the Astral Sea as we speak. We need to arm our bomb and close this portal before it is too late! Quick, take these Infernal Detonators and place them around the portal while we continue configuring the bomb!”

Alexandro points to a chest in which there are four infernal detonators. “Place these at four points around the portal, but be careful, it is still unstable at this time!” The Stacked Deck places the detonators.

As they do so, General de la Sern takes Bobby aside: “I do not know who along your crew we can truly trust, but I must put some faith in at least one of you, and I remember you as most fortuitous when last we met at Isla Ruz. I fear that simply detonating this bomb will not truly rid us of our problems, but merely delay them. I am entrusting you with this device, which can ensure that this portal here at the manor never reopens. Plant this within the astral conduit before you depart, but be wary that it not fall into any traitor’s hands, and do not place it too quickly, as you will have limited time to escape yourselves before it activates—I expect you will only have a few minutes to get out after you place it in the conduit!" He hands Bobby a glowing device with glowing runes in some ancient dead language, that can fit within his pocket.

The party then realized that the king may be in the portal with Batista, and tell Alejandro. He replies, King Antonino may be many things, but at his heart he is a friend of all Tiialia, including the Isla Ruz. We owe it to our king to give you a chance to retrieve him from Zalvidar before we ignite the Infernal Bomb. We can hold off the yuan-ti here for a short time while you all enter the portal to save the king. Jaemee – the Astral Spelljammer that you designed for your dissertation – surely it is still in your greater bag of holding? If ever there were a time to use it, it’s now!"

Jaemee pulls his dissertation spelljammer ship out of his greater bag of holding, and the party departs.

**Challenge 1**
As Bobby gazed into the astral sea, he swears he sees a shape drifting in empty space. He focuses his eyes and sees his fiancé, [[Blotta Balmer]].

He tries to rope her in, but Hector is not bought in, while the Knight assists Bobby. Murphy tells Bobby to drop the rope and he does, so Blotta falls to oblivion.

Right behind them, a mysterious figure appears. Their eyes burn a shade of blue more vibrant than you can comprehend. They look at Bobby and say “While others may have betrayed them, your love for Blotta still burns. May your fire never dim.” Bobby suddenly finds himself holding an alchemist’s fire in each hand. As he ponders this, the mysterious stranger points at "Playing Card" Murphy and says “When the deck is stacked for all who see, it is the blind man who truly has the advantage.” As she finishes, they hear Jaemee from the back yelling “Astral Storm!” and you each find yourselves engulfed in a spectrum of flashing colors beyond your comprehension.

**Challenge 2**
As the colors subside, they find themselves placed around a great table made of pure, shining crystal. Outside of some casual observers, you spot some familiar faces. They see…

- Big Thistle, whose party’s spot in the Gran Coliseo de la Ruz the Stacked Deck stole to gain access to the Ruzian leaders.
- Mervin “The Mayor” Bissell – the fixer for the Batista family who you murdered at Roberto’s birthday party and whose note you then stole which described the coming Batistan invasion of Isla Ruz.
- The Acolyte of New Corrodreth, whose disguise as King Esposito was exposed by the Stacked Deck (who subsequently murdered him).
- And of course, [[Falsus Omnibus]], who originally sent you to conduct a heist to steal the Tiialan Stones at [[Roberto Batista]]’s party, but from whom you took the stones after he betrayed your party.
- Verum Omnibus, Falsus’ honest twin brother, is dealing.

Falsus Omnibus says “Well, Playing Card, it seems we have all arrived for our house game of Liar’s Cards. It will be you against me. You know the rules…There are six types of cards you can pull: (1) the tin cat, (2) the copper newt, (3) the bronze chameleon, (4) the silver snake, (5) the golden sphinx, and (6) the platinum dog. Declare a number and amount that you believe exists across all hands. Each round, the player must either bid a higher quantity of the same card, or any quantity of a higher card, or challenge the previous bid to end the round. The players indicate the amount they had of the card at play. If the bid is valid (there were at least as many of that card in play, then the bidder wins. Otherwise, the challenger wins. The loser loses one die for the next round.

Falsus easily wins the first round, almost too easily, and says “Let’s make this interesting. I’ll raise you this next round this mysterious pack of magical cards that I have acquired. And perhaps you could raise, oh I don’t know, that Tiialan Stone you STOLE FROM ME? What do you say, Playing Card?”

Murphy susses out how Falsus might be cheating, while Bobby tells him to not look at his hand and Hector prepares to attack the crowd. Murphy chooses not to look at his hand, and is able to call Falsus on his bluff in the second round and win. Murphy earns a Deck of Oracles.

**Challenge 3**
The group is transported to a familiar reception hall. Some of them recall this hall from their heist on Roberto Batista’s birthday. Music is playing loudly in the hall from some performing maestros. Lady Algonza Toronado appears swamped by both Ren Sniffo and Quentin Chowders. She looks very uncomfortable. But this is on the other end of the dance floor from where you are at. What will they do?

The mysterious emissary briefly appears in front of The Knight of Confidence, saying “The lady is accosted by these ruffians. If only she had a confident man to cast them away and arouse her spirits.” She then fades into the visage of Dean Jacksons, who is speaking to the Knight of Confidence: “You just gotta roll with the punches, you feel me, my main man?”

The group eventually convinces the Knight of Confidence to help Algonza. He bumps into Quentin and Ren to break them off. The lady was impressed and opens up her shop on top of giving the Knight of Confidence a Potion of Heroism.

The room again begins to swirl with the colorful magic of the Astral storm. A noble lady takes out her hand and asks Hector for a dance. As they begin to dance, her face shifts into that of the Mysterious Emissary. She leans to him and gestures toward Playing Card, The Knight of Confidence, and Bobby. “Remember that sometimes the blood of the covenant may run thicker than the water of the womb…” She disappears into the swirl of colors as new scene takes place before Hector's eyes eyes.

**Challenge 4**
The group find themselves in one of the great courtrooms of Nova Jersaeria. Seated in the judge’s seat is Ambassador Tiny Timmy Treble, royal representative of the king, and before him, bound and surrounded by guards, stand [[Faustia Fanto-Domingo]] and Nico Fanto-Domingo.
Tiny Timmy Treble: “So, these clowns are brought before the court today because they betrayed the trust of all of Tiallia. What say you both?
Faustia: “I died serving the true lineage of Tiallia, the Fanto-Domingo lineage!”
Nico: “Esposito, more like Espo-fuck your mother, you know what I’m saying? And frankly, sources tell me that [[Lord Draco Landucci|Lord Landucci]] is stable, please God. When this is all over the Fanto-Domingos are gonna be dancing on your grave and the grave of any soldier (or child for that matter) that gets in our way!”
The group gets the sense that these Fanto-Domingos are totally unrepentant in their ways and still seek to undermine the Tiiallan order.
Treble: “Does anyone here today have any words to speak for or against these accused conspirators? If you see honor or treachery in their words, speak now or allow the justice of Tiallia to fall upon them!”
Hector reluctantly chooses Tiialia over his cousins.

Ambassador Treble: "Listen, I’m not one to hold a kid responsible for the actions of his family—God knows my uncle didn’t do me or his four wives any favor. Imma be real with you, kid, Tiialia needs some stability, and we could use a real Fanto-Domingo that’s willing to be a team player for the king. That’s why I’ve decided to grant you a pardon for your family’s crimes and declare you as the new head of House Fanto-Domingo. All that’s missing is the signature of the king.”

Ambassador Treble leans in toward Hector and hands him the pardon “So, uh, do you think you and your little crew could get that taken care of, kid?”' As he says this, the scene swirls into a myriad of colors. The adventurers hear Jaemee yelling “Alright, folks, looks like we’re clear of that astral storm, is everyone alright?”

**The Ascension**
The party reappears on Jaemee’s Astral Dissertation Ship, and see a looming Astral Island in front of them, built upon the corpse of a dead god, with ancient, largely ruinous, buildings casting a dark shadow over the Astral sea. Toward the center of the island is a large cathedral-like building of pure black, through which they can see a shimmering of lights. They also notice a chest that they do not recall from before, which contains within it four robes of Batista black and red.

They enter into the upper story overlooking a large hall, along these long balconies they find several other figure wearing the same cloaks as them all. At one end of the hall they see a large, pulsing artifact. At the other end of the hall, they see Antonino Esposito, restrained to a large slab, with none other than the Eternal Lord Zalvidar Batista standing quietly behind the slab. Standing in front of the slab, is a figure which some in the party take a moment to recognize. It is none other than the largest, most Reanimated Corpse of [[Carlos 'Lucky' Luciano|Carlos "Lucky" Luciano]]—the famous bruiser for the Batista Family that has now been killed twice by The Stacked Deck. To his right, hovering by a large switch, stands an imposing yuan-ti not dissimilar from the one that was imitating Mayor Connolly in Batistallas. He speaks as they enter the room.

“Tonight we honor the ancient bonds that unite the the Eternal Lord Batista and Corrodreth Reborn—a bond born in millennia past in the land of [[Dreth]]! The Eternal Lord has bestowed on us an Astral Conduit that will connect our banished brethren directly onto the unsuspecting and unworthy so called “Kingdom of Tiialia.” And in exchange, we have delivered unto the Eternal Lord Tiialia’s King so that his life force might sustain the Eternal Lord. Let the transference BEGIN!”

And with that, the yuan-ti slams down on the lever, which emits a large thunderous boom as light being to emanate between Lord Batista and King Esposito. As this happens the Eternal Lord Batista’s eyes close as if in trance, and he begins to rise off the floor, suspended in a dazzling light that seems to roll between him and the King like waves. The Lord keeps siphoning HP from the King until this is resolved.

The Stacked Deck make short work of their enemies, killing , the acolytes, the yuan-ti, and Lord Zalvadar. They turn off the transfer lever and save the king.

As they flee on Jaemee’s ship, they see the pulsing light coming from the astral conduit begin to shift in color, from blue to violet. Jaemee yells, “I’m thinking really hard of Selerim, fellas, hang on!"

**Far East**
As they regain consciousness, The Stacked Deck, along with King Antonino Esposito (who signs Hector's pardon) find themselves lost. They then encounter [[Myrrdin Turftoe]] who tells them that this mysterious portal has been redirected, along with them to the [[Tallwoods]].

It seems the party has saved Tiialia from immanent invasion but left the Tallwoods and surrounding country exposed.
