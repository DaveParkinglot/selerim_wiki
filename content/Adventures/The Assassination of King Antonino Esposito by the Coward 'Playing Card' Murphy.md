## Adventure Premise

Word has spread across [[Tiialia]] that [[Antonino Esposito|King Antonino Esposito]] is visiting [[Batistallas]], a major city under control of the [[Batista Family]], to shore up unity across the realm. But with the Batista and [[Ruz Family|Ruz]] families on the brink of war, and with rumors about impending incursions by the forces of [[Morganth]] or even [[Vlorngroth]], these are dangerous times in Tiialia.

You have been summoned to the _Carousel Club_ in downtown Batistallas at urgent request of Ambassador “Tiny” Timmy Trebles and [[Falsus Omnibus]], who have relayed the following message:
“King Esposito in grave danger. [['Playing Card' Murphy]] is hired assassin. Save the king and save Tiallia. Meet at Carousel Club for instructions.”

Will you heed the call and save the Kingdom of Tiallia, or will you end up the first casualties in the downfall of the Esposito lineage?

## Outcomes

### Session 1

- [[Hector Fanto-Domingo|Hector]] and the [[Knight of Confidence]] met at the entrance to Batistallas after receiving their summons. As they walked into the city, they were summoned off to a side street by none other than "Playing Card" Murphy, who is under the impression that he simply has to lay low for a few days in order to win a prize, as described in this message he received:
  - Outside: CONGRATULATIONS, YOU’VE WON (TIME SENSITIVE REWARD INSIDE)! Inside: Congratulations, Mr. Murphy! You have been selected for the “Raise the Roof” award in Batistallas! To participate, arrive at the roof of the Batista Church Depository in Batistallas at midday, Mourne 22nd. If you do this, you will be presented with 20,000 gold pieces or its equivalent in game chips at the fabulous Carousel Club! Sincerely, The Committee of Innovative Awards

- As Murphy is explaining the situation, the group is descended upon by some city watch who turn out to be imposters. They make swift work of these imposters but are seen by citizens of the city supposedly killing members of the city watch, and thus are branded as wanted.
  ![[z_assets/assassination_wanted_poster.png|300]]

- The party lays low trying to figure out what is going on with the note Murphy received. They engage in a scouting mission to figure out the what, how, and why of this supposed plot to assassinate the king.

- **Round 1 of the investigation**
  - Hector first investigates the Abandoned Yuan-ti Keep, as he things the king might be a snake guy. In a shocking turn of events, he finds a large snakelike shed skin, as well as a slithering path with multiple sets of humanoid footprints heading from the old keep toward the city.
  - The Knight of Confidence investigates BT\&T Arena and sees that security is always high around BT\&T Arena, especially after the stampedes following [[Nova Jersaeria]]’s win over Batistallas in the Batistaball World Cup last season. There is no game scheduled for the day of the King’s visit, but he imagines this area will be locked down. Unless Batistallas’s star Batistaball player, Belé, is in on the plot to assassinate the king, he is pretty sure the conspiracy does not pass through the sports arena. However, he does find a plaque by the arena, dedicated “To the Band of Batista, the mysterious company of soldiers who arrived at the end of the siege of the [[Corrodreth|Salted City]], the veterans of whom were gifted this land and founded Batistallas after the siege concluded.” What interesting lore!
  - "Playing Card" Murphy goes to the River Walk, pretending to be a street performer. While security seems tight along this side of the river, looking across to where the parade route continues to the eastern end of the city across the Zalvidar Bridge, the city guard presence seems to taper off. He encounters a fisherman wearing a Make All Tiialia Awesome hat. Upon questioning he is quick to share about how he saw two Ruzians talking with one another all suspicious-like just the other day. He thinks that he heard the words “Fake King Esposito” and “Grassy Knoll.”

- \*\*Round 2 of the investigation
  - Hector then goes to the Zalvidar Batista Lordship Library. A transcription from one of the “Batista Tapes” catches his eye – a conversation some time ago between Falsus Omnibus and Roberto Batista:
    - Batista: New performer I saw coming through Tiialia – calls himself the Salty Boy. Apparently doing shows at every military camp in the kingdom. Says he’s with the Committee for Innovative Awards. Wears a lot of leather. I swear his eyes had a vertical slit for a pupil. A bit odd but a real |expletive| show.
    - Omnibus: |inaudible|
    - Batista: True. They run the banks, too, you know.
  - The Knight of Confidence goes to Bastista Square. He encounters a fruit seller manning his stand on the edge of the square that sees his inquisitive eyes and gestures him over. He tells him how, as he was sleeping in his fruit stand in the early morning—do not criticize him, he is a working man, what would you know about that—he awoke to some commotion. He swears that he saw a yuan-ti slithering by along with several other individuals.
  - Murphy goes to the Reunion Tower and pretends to be a janitor to get in. This tower is full of tourists, clearly in town for the royal parade. A lot of them are wearing “Make All Tiialia Awesome” themed gear. He overhears many conversations about how the Batistas should kick out all Ruzians from the city and finally end the decades long imminent threat of family Ruz on Tiialia. They see spatterings of the Flag of Tiialia throughout the city from this vantage point. In the shade of the Bleeding Peaks, the flag feels almost foreign, unwelcome here. As if some ancient force is looming over the kingdom of Tiialia, ready to pounce. Just honestly not great weather for the tower today, and they wish they could get a refund for their expensive tickets.

- **Round 3 of the investigation**
  - Hector goes to the Batista Royal Manor. He sees a seemingly city-sanctioned burning of an effigy that seems to be in the likeness of [[Alejandro Ruz]]. It appears that tensions are boiling over in Tiialia after the recent events in Isla Ruz. In a crowd of Batistallasites burning a Ruzian effigy, he encounters one angry civilians who yells at you that “those Ruzians are trying to kill our king! We need Zalvidar in command! The whole Ruzian civilization will die!
  - The Knight of Confidence goes to the Tiialian Royal Diplomatic Corps. That’s odd…this is presumably the Tiialian Royal Diplomatic Corp, but they see only Batista guards stationed outside. They chat with a diplomat across the street. They mention how excited they were when the Corps opened up just recently, but suddenly it seems like someone is working to dismantle the newfound royal attempt to excerpt more control on Batistallas. It seems really quite anti-Tiiallan
  - Murphy goes to the Ruzian Trading Post. He speaks to a Ruzian merchant, asking about the king’s security on his parade. He begins ranting about how the sharp turn at Batistallas Plaza right before “the bastard Zalvidar” library puts has too many angles from which to protect the king. A Ruzian guard at the trade post quips about how he expects they’ll find “the dumbest, gambling-obsessed, debt-laden bum” possible as a patsy for whatever false flag the Batistas are surely planning against the great people of Ruz.

- They then head to the Carousel Club to figure out what is up. The see Lady Algonza, who they remember from their past voyages. She is upset that the king seemingly left his royal caravan (with which she was traveling) early in the morning as they were approaching Batistallas, and she has not seen him since. They also encounter Dean Jacksons, the hip owner of the club, who ushers them to a private room, where they find Mr. X, a mysterious character who seems to know a lot about what is going on. He conveys the following monologue:
  - **_Mr X:_** _I’m not with the Committee of Innovative Awards, Mr. 'Playing Card' and company, and I assume if you’ve come this far, what I have to say interests you. But I’m not going to name names, or tell you who or what I represent. Except to say… You’re close, you’re closer than you think…_
  - \_One of my routine duties if I had been assigned here in Batistallas would’ve been to arrange for additional security. The Royal Guard is relatively small, and by custom the military will augment them. I checked it out and sure enough, I found out someone has told the 112th Military Intelligence Group at 4th Army Headquarters at Fort Scallion to « stand down » in the coming days day, over the protests of the unit Commander, a Colonel [[Carbuckle Duskshot]] … Now this is significant, because it is standard operating procedure, especially in a known hostile city like Batistallas, to supplement the Royal Guard. Even if we had not allowed the bubbletop to be removed from the royal carriage, we’d put at least 100 to 200 soldiers on the sidewalks, without question! There’s already been several attempts on [[Hugh Mann]]'s life in [[Scrimshaw City|Scrimshaw]]. Only a month before in Batistallas, Ambassador Tiny Timmy Treble had been spit on and hit. We should have arrived days ahead of time, studied the route, checked all the buildings … We’d never allow all these wide-open empty windows overlooking Batistallas Plaza … never … We should have our own bowmen covering the area. The moment a window would go up they should be on the sending stone. We should be watching the crowds - packages, rolled up newspapers, a coat over an arm. You should feel an Army presence in the streets today, but none of this has happened. It is a violation of the most basic protection codes we have. And it’s the best indication of a massive plot in Batistallas.
  - _Who could do that? People in my business, Mr. Playing Card. You see, I don’t think that the individual about to be assassinated is King Esposito at all. I think this is all a ruse to kick off a war and knock the Ruzians out of business in one fell swoop, and to shut down whatever reforms King Esposito might be planning. I think, under the rouse of stopping a Ruzian revolution, plans are being laid for something far more sinister._
  - _That’s the real question, isn’t it-« Why? »-the « how » and the who is just « scenery » for the suckers._
  - _Kings are killed, Mr. Playing Card. Politics is power, nothing more. But don’t believe me. Don’t trust me. Do your own work, your own thinking.\__
  - \*Here’s what I can tell you. You have friends, but to find them, you’ll need to be close by to where they’re hiding. The Ruzians have only told me that “the pride comes before the fall.” Keep that in mind and perhaps you may have the help you need.

### Session 2

- [[Player Characters/The Stacked Deck/index|The Stacked Deck]] head to the Batista Church Depository to try and stop the impending assassination. They have only a clue about (presumably) a "Grassy Knoll" and "the pride comes before the fall"
- **The carriage is en route**
  - They go seeking answers. The Knight of Confidence barges into a storeroom and finds [[Juice Johnson]], who indicates that the Batistas are trying to frame the Ruz Family for the assassination of the King Esposito, and that that the king in the carriage is actually a yuan-ti imposter; the real king was delayed or kidnapped as per Lady Algonza's comments.
  - Hector goes into a nearby tavern and finds Dean Jacksons, the owner of the Carousel Club. Now that he is away from prying eyes, he tells Hector that "the pride comes before the fall." Hector is dumb and confused, and ultimately, due to the wild magic of the Bleeding Peaks, somehow manages to get the temporary ability to pass through walls, which he uses to try and steal a hoagie (and fails). Eventually sees a fallen commoner next to a prideful commoner, both of whom are by some suspicious shrubbery near the main road. Hiding in the shrubbery he finds the Grassy Gnoll, a gnoll with a gun. The gnoll says he would "never kill the real king" and that the person in the carriage coming through is not the king. The gnoll wants to save the kingdom from the "New [[Corrodreth]] Hegemony."
  - Playing Card Murphy heads into the Batista Church Depository and finds none other than Falsus Omnibus in the back room. Falsus tells him that yes, he is definitely a patsy, but does he really want to give up the chance at all that gold? He implores him to go upstairs. Playing Card drags Falsus up the stairs with him, but after being struck with temporary madness from the Bleeding Peaks, comes to believe that Falsus is actually a baby that he is holding. He ultimately runs away from the roof, as the king's carriage is heard approaching.
- **Round 2**\*
  - The King's carriage then comes around the corner, with Mayor Connolly of Batistallas. The Grassy Gnoll says that the mayor is also a yuan-ti, and is holding the illusion spell over the carriage. The Stacked Deck position Playing Card to take the shot on Mayor Connolly, who is holding the illusion spell of the snake king in place. Grassy Gnoll hands a Magic Arrow (Arrow of Slaying) to both the Knight of Confidence and Playing Card.
  - Playing Card takes the shot with his Magic Arrow, and blasts the fake mayor, revealing that the king, the mayor, and their guards are actually yuan-ti.
  - Battle ensues and the Stacked Deck defeat the yuan-ti, who kept screaming something about a "New Corrodreth."
  - Just as the group is about to savor their victory, they are approached by a staggering and bleeding Ambassador Tiny Timmy Treble. He hands a note to the group as he collapses, having seemingly been stabbed many times. The note reads:
    - _King Antonino alive. Held captive in Zalvidar Batista’s Mountain Retreat. He can be rescued! Don’t stop believing!_
  - The crowd is extremely confused about what they just witnessed. Are these those wanted murderers? Did they just kill the king? Was the king secretly a snake person?
  - Chaos erupts as crowds flee and The Stacked Deck realize that, while they may have foiled the attempt to frame Playing Card Murphy for a staged assassination, on the king, they are still in a fight against time to rescue the real King Antonino Esposito.
  - What will they do next?
