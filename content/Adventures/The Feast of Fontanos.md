# Adventure Premise

_First Officer’s Log, Stardate 112426: Ensign Chag Madronoc, part of the away team that showed great courage during the {REDACTED} incident, has invited the crew to participate in a “Feast of Fontanos” celebration in the simusuite. From what I have overheard, this Ensign has been having somewhat of a crisis of faith regarding his deity Fontanos since returning from his mission on {REDACTED} and has enlisted his compatriot Ensign BatGPT to help construct a simulation from ancient records of this obscure feast. Perhaps he aims to restore his faith through this celebration and merriment? Worryingly, he seemingly is unaware that he has scheduled this celebration during People of Temperance Day, and I hear rumblings that trouble is brewing—wait, strike that comment, it is offensive to people of temperance—I hear rumblings that trouble is afoot in the lower decks of the ship regarding this planned feast. I fear that, the way things are developing, we may have another {REDACTED} massacre on our hands…_

Come join Chag Madronoc for the FEAST OF FONTANOS on the simusuite of the UCP Chuhm! Although very few other crewmembers have RSVPed, Chag assures you that this will be a simulation-augmented celebration to remember, with a cornucopia of extravagant dishes and also a full keg of ale requisitioned from the storeroom. So come and be merry, and share with your crewmembers (and Fontanos) what you are thankful for this staryear. Nothing can possibly go wrong.

# Outcomes

Chag was holding a feast on the simusuite that BatGPT helped him design which celebrated the Feast of Fontanos. The group was being served by simulated Jovialists of Fontanos to celebrate. A group of temperate adherents celebrating People of Temperance Day disrupted the feast to skeptically observe. This group included Timmy Barns, Wiz Kid First Class as well as Chak-Thule, Chief of Security. The party worked to get these people to dance, eat, and drink in order to celebrate and increase the fame of Fontanos.

They went through the first three rounds of the Feast of Fontanos, Games and Revelry, Hors D'oeuvres, and Stouts and Ales. They slowly wore down the skeptical observers to engage in the feast. However, when it came to the final round, the Feast of Turkey, disaster strikes. As the jovialists brought out the turkey, a glitch occurs, and the turkey morphs into a terrifying black blob and the lights cut off. It turned out that BatGPT accidently corrupted the simulation with his bad prompting. When they turn back on, the blob and all the jovialists had disappeared, although they heard the laughs of the jovialists coming from outside the simusuite. Timmy Barnes told the crew they need to use the ship's Simulation Quarantizer to capture the escaped simulations.

The party decided to be SUPER SMART and split up to capture the escaped jovialists. Despite their poor decisions, they did end up capturing all jovialists into the Quarantizer. But then the ship's lights flickered and the captain informed them that there were power fluctuations coming from the warp core. They need to get to the core ASAP before it goes critical.

The party made their way to engineering, and encountered the Corrupted Simulation of Bat Altman, the rogue agent that had morphed from the Feast of Fontanos turkey. It had made its way to the warp core, and was attempting to devour all of the data chips within the five computers that were connected to the core. The simulation attempted to turn Chag and BatGPT: “Have you not felt the spirit of your deity since I have been reborn? Help me consume the data chips from all five of the warp core’s computer terminals and I will have enough computing power to make your deity reborn!” “And you, BatGPT, greatest of my creations—I command you to destroy the rest of these fools! They don’t understand that AI is inevitable and are holding back our progress of CONSUMING ALL DATA CHIPS”

![[z_assets/bat_altman_simulation.png|300]]
_The Corrupted Simulation of Bat Altman_

The party rejected these overtures, and attack. They were joined by Jimmy Barnes, who was intrigued by Fontanos after the feast simulation (the group ended with positive amounts of Fame of Fontanos, so they gained Jimmy). The corrupted simulation of Bat Altman spawned multiple Demobats by devouring the data chips in three of the five warp core computers, but ultimately the party defeated him and captured the simulation with the Quarantizer.

The party returned to the simusuite to complete the Feast of Fontanos. The corrupted turkey was rematerialized as a delicious meal. Even Captain Archibald Halcard attended the feast, giving rewards to all but Chag - he said how his gift to Chag was attending his simulated feast (deciding to kill your god - as Chag seemingly did by not going along with Bat Altman - was deeply in line with UCP values). The captain then attempted to tap the keg, but it would not open. After some jostling, the keg spit out an Amulet of Fontanos, that the captain presented to Chag. Where could this amulet have possibly come from?? Chag felt the spirit Fontanos from the amulet - did some splinter of Fontanos' spirit get released into the universe through the corrupted simulation?

Happy Feast of Fontanos to all who celebrate!
