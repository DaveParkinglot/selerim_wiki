# Overview

Formerly a crewmate of the famous [[Captain Winchester Bone|Captain Bone]], Purt is a native of [[Mitlin]] who has not made the most of his life.

Since exiting the employment of Captain Bone, Purt has become addicted to "[[Ancient Dragon Marrow|The Substance]]". He also moonlights as a barkeep at Longwood Tavern, while running a loan shark business on the side.
