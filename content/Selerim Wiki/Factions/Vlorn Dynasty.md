After the god [Selvetarm](https://5e.tools/deities.html#selvetarm_drow_scag) was forever banished to the Infinite Abyss, his surviving followers retreated far beneath the world's surface into the subterranean realm known as the Underdark.

Only in recent centuries have these remnants united under a single banner, calling themselves the Vlorn Dynasty.
