**Date:** [[Selerim Calendar|Khorg]] 1st - Khorg 15th

Holiday celebrated mainly by dwarves. Traditionally, dwarves fire their forges up on this day and do not turn them off until winter begins to ebb. Beginning on Khorg 1st, dwarves celebrate for 2 weeks' time.
