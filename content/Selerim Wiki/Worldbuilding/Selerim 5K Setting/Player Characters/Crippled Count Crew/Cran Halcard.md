Cran Halcard is a man whose reputation in the underworld is as complex as the intricate routes he once navigated through the stars. A skilled pilot and smuggler, Cran built his career around delivering goods to places where the law didn’t dare venture, all while maintaining a certain moral code — a code that ultimately led to his downfall. Born to a family of outcasts on [[Eurythia III]], a backwater colony on the fringes of civilized space, Cran learned early how to survive by his wits and his hands.

His natural aptitude for piloting ships earned him a reputation as a top-tier navigator, and soon enough, he was recruited by some of the most notorious crime syndicates in the galaxy. For a time, he was just another face in the smuggling game, moving illicit goods without question. But one fateful shipment would change everything. He is now laying low after running afoul of notorious crime boss [[Capo Gusanno]], constantly moving from one dead-end job to the next, always a step ahead of the bounty hunters sent by Gusanno.
