Title: The High Rollers
---

An adventuring party formed in the sleepy fishing village of [[Crabbley]]. They saved the world on several occasions, garnering much fame and adoration through the [[Summer Lands]].

At the peak of their powers, they appeared invincible, but they mysteriously vanished in the Spring of 264. As it turned out, their attempt to infiltrate the Fathomless Vault of [[Tzastis the Collector]] led to their capture. Archfey Tzastis held them captive as trophies for over two years, until they were [[Escape from the Fathomless Vault| rescued]] by a [[Player Characters/Bobby's Boys/index|new group of adventurers]] sponsored by former High Roller [[Hugh Mann]].

All of their combined heroism, misadventures, and mayhem are cataloged here: [[The High Rollers Campaign]].
