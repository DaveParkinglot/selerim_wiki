![[z_assets/blackfyre_orb.png]]

These powerful, abyssal artifacts are known to be used in ritualistic summoning, though they likely contain other magical properties.

The first recorded use of a Blackfyre Orb in recent times was an attack in the heartland of [[Factions/Kingdom of Tiialia/index|Tiialia]]. A demon was summoned by the dreadful forces of [[Morganth]], but the incursion was thwarted by [[Dunland's Raiders]].

Another Blackfyre Orb was used to summon [[Proklor the Despoiler]] into [[Vlorngroth - The Manor of Madness|The Manor of Madness]] in the subterranean city of [[Vlorngroth]].

A third orb, located in the ruined city of [[Corrodreth]], was retrieved by the [[Legion of Doom]].

Rumor has it that Vlorngroth's [[High Lord Kranicz]] seeks to gather Blackfyre Orbs, and he is paying a hefty price for them.
