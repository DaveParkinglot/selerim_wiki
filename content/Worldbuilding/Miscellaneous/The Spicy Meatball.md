Local Tiialan eatery in [[Scrimshaw City|Scrimshaw]], located in the alleyway where [[Terry the Terrible]] and his gang used to hang out. [[The High Rollers]] renovated the place and opened the restaurant with the help of their friends.

With the disappearance of the adventuring party, their friends still run the joint, with [[Loeb]] acting as the boss.
