This civil war was fought between the 4 major noble families of [[Factions/Kingdom of Tiialia/index|Tiialia]]:

- [[Adonzio Family]]
- [[Fanto-Domingo Family]]
- [[Landucci Family]]
- [[Esposito Family]]

King Seraphino, last king of Tiialia, was slain near the end of the [[Serpent Wars]], during the First Siege of [[Corrodreth]]. For the better part of a century, a series of regent lords held the throne of Tiialia. This ended with the rule of Aleemus the Aged.

After Aleemus' death, the War of the Four Families began, lasting for 3 years. Originally it was a free for all between the families, but this changed after the Midsummer Massacre, when the [[Fanto-Domingo Family]] wiped out the [[Esposito Family]]. This led to an alliance between the Adonzios and the Landuccis, which became known as the Adonzio Accord. As the broker of the peace, it was decided that Antonio Adonzio, patriarch of the Adonzios, would take the throne of Tiialia.
