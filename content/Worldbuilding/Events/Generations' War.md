- A long, grinding war of attrition war fought thousands of years ago between the great human and elvish empires of the [[Inhabitable Lands]].
- Major causes include human resentment of the elvish gods ([[The Seldarine]]) and the human champion [[Barbanus the Slayer]] hunting and killing the great dragon [[Timphiliax]].
- The ancient battlefields of this war are mostly buried beneath the waves, as it was fought before [[Darkwells]] erupted.
- The Sunken Plain in the far west is thought to have been caused by this conflict.
