A long, grinding war of attrition war fought thousands of years ago between the great human and elvish empires of the [[Summer Lands]]. Major causes include human resentment of the elvish gods the [Seldarine](https://5e.tools/tables.html#elf%20deities%20\(the%20seldarine\)_mtf), and the human champion [[Barbanus the Slayer]]'s hunting and killing of the great dragon [[Timphiliax]].

The ancient battlefields of this war are mostly buried beneath the waves, as it was fought two centuries before the [[Darkwells]] erupted.
