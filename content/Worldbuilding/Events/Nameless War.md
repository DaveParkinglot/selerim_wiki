Early in Selerim's history, 7,500 years before [[Factions/Tiialia]]'s founding, this brutal war raged between [The Seldarine](https://5e.tools/tables.html#elf%20deities%20\(the%20seldarine\)_mtf) and [The Dark Seldarine](https://5e.tools/tables.html#drow%20deities%20\(the%20dark%20seldarine\)_mtf). It lasted a millennia and a half, claimed an uncountable number of lives, ravaged the world, and destroyed all the moons except for one–[[Lunadel]].
