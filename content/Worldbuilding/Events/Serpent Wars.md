A long, drawn out, series of wars fought for 11 years between [[Factions/Kingdom of Tiialia/index|Tiialia]] and the ancient yuan-ti city state of [[Corrodreth]].

There were two notable sieges laid before the walls of Corrodreth—Tiialans know them as the First Siege and the Second Siege:

- In the First Siege, King Seraphino of Tiialia was slain along with his royal guard and his champion, Grand Crusader Hectonius Domingo.
- After the First Siege, [[Factions/Kingdom of Tiialia/index|Tiialia]] rallied its legions and its artillery mages for the Second Siege of [[Corrodreth]]. The city was bombarded, pillaged, destroyed, and finally salted to avenge King Seraphino, the Last King of [[Factions/Kingdom of Tiialia/index|Tiialia]].
