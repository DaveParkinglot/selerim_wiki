**Date:** [[Selerim Calendar|Mourne]] 1st

Also called Samhain by Fey folk, this holiday is a feast day of the dead, and a celebration of the harvest. Celebrations include pumpkin carving, tricks & treats, apple bobbing, and scary stories.
