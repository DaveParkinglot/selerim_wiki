**Date:** [[Selerim Calendar|Bahax]] 10th

Commemorates the great platinum dragon god [Bahamut](https://5e.tools/deities.html#bahamut_exandria_egw). The date 10/10 was chosen due to its association with order and law, which are Bahamut's domains.
