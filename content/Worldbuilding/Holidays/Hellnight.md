**Date:** [[Selerim Calendar|Sel]] 20th

A devil's favorite holiday, celebrated on the equinox as spring fades into summer, the season of sin. Everyone knows that more crimes happen in the summer, but also, mortals sign more Infernal Contracts in the summer months than all the other months combined!
