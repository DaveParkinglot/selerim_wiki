**Date:** [[Selerim Calendar|Japel]] 1st

A day to celebrate tricks, jests, and fools. Celebrated originally by goblins of the Feywild. In the Feywild, the holiday is celebrated with the yearly prank-filled [[Festival of Japes]], near the forest west of the [[Jade Queen]]'s domain.
