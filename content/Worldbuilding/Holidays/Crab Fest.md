**Date:** [[Selerim Calendar|Fiersel]] 10th

A festival celebrating the lowly, yet ever-versatile crab, held each year on the 10th of Fiersel. Usually the festival was held in an open air venue at the [[Cult of the Crab]], but after the attack by [[Malmstein the Tangler]] and his [[Tanglers]], the festival began introducing security measures.
