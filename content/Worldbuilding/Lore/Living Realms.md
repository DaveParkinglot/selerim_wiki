- The select few pockets of Selerim that can support sapient life, they are akin to bubbles of safety within a raging storm.
- Most of these realms are free from the deadly arcane fallout of the [[Nameless War]].
- The unlivable regions of the world have never been mapped because they are both uninhabitable and deadly to those who tread there.
- The scope and number of the Living Realms is not yet known, and has yet to be recorded.
- The largest, most documented, and most notable of the realms is the [[Summer Lands]].
