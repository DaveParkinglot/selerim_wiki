Far far into the future, the [[Factions/Kingdom of Tiialia/index|Kingdom of Tiialia]] has taken great bounds into the stars.

Forged in the astral fires of the [[War of Eternal Unification]], the empire holds a tight grip on both the Outer Rim and the [[Outer Outer Rim]].

[[Super Nova Jersaeria]], a supermassive space station, is the empire's capital city.
