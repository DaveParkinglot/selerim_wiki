Named for the ancient city [[Nova Jersaeria]], Super Nova Jersaeria is a massive space station that doubles as the capital city for the [[Astral Empire of Tiialia]]. Its power is provided entirely by a highly unstable, captive fragment of a dead god

The city tends to loom close to Selerim, capital of the Astral Empire, though it never orbits one planet for too long.
