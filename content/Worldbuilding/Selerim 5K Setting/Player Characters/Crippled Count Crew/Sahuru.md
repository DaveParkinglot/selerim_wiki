When the Sahuru's race underwent the Biotransference, Sahuru was stripped of its soul, leaving only a whisper of his former self. Yet unlike many of his kin, something within him resisted complete erasure. A faint flicker of his former glory and honor remained, buried beneath cold, unyielding logic. For countless millennia, he stood in silent vigilance as the Great Sleep consumed his race, entombed within a forgotten Tomb World lost to time. Something disrupted Sahuru's reawakening from the Great Sleep. When he emerged from his stasis, he found himself alone. His tomb world in ruins, and his masters either destroyed or scattered across the stars. His once-unshakable purpose faltered. Where once he only sought to serve his own dynasty, he now seeks to find those of his own race and rejoin his endless watch.
