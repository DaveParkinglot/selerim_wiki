Like most [[Bopus Bots]], Aux specializes in janitorial duties. Like all Bopus Bots, he was crafted in the grand image of his deity and patron [[Bopus]], the Machine God. However, unlike most Bopus Bots, Aux has successfully completed 50 years of service with no defective rampancy. This has earned Aux the freedom and privilege to pursue adventure, along with a Red Hat, which allows him to commune with his god. Bopus himself encourages Red Hatted bots to seek out adventures, and so Aux applied for the first job posting he saw on the notice board, and he is now preparing to ship out to [[Chauntea's Rest]].
